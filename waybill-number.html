Where to find your JRS Express waybill number

A waybill is a shipping document, and its reference number can be used to look up shipment information.

Check your shipping receipt

Start with the receipt or document given to the sender when the parcel was booked. Examine the printed labels and look for a field identified as a waybill, airbill, shipment, or tracking reference. The exact layout can vary, so read the nearby labels rather than relying only on the position of the number.

Copy the reference accurately

  • Include all letters and digits that belong to the reference.
  • Do not add punctuation or spaces unless they appear as part of the number.
  • Check similar-looking characters before submitting it.
  • Keep the original receipt in case you need assistance.

What if you cannot find it?

Ask the sender for the shipment reference or check the booking confirmation they received. If the receipt is damaged or the number is unreadable, contact the shipping provider using its official support channels.

Use the number to check for updates

Once you have the correct reference, enter it into a tracking service such as the JRS Tracking tool. Treat the displayed result as tracking information, and contact JRS Express for official clarification about a specific shipment.

Protect shipment details. A waybill reference may reveal information about a parcel. Share it only with the sender, recipient, or authorized support staff.
