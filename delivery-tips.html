Before and during delivery

Practical parcel delivery tips

A few simple habits can make it easier to follow a shipment and resolve questions if something goes wrong.

For senders

  • Write the recipient's name and delivery details clearly.
  • Check the address and contact number with the recipient before shipping.
  • Package the item appropriately for its contents.
  • Keep the receipt and share the tracking reference privately with the recipient.

For recipients

  • Keep the sender's shipment reference available.
  • Check tracking updates through a tracking service.
  • Make sure someone can receive the parcel where appropriate.
  • Use official carrier channels to confirm delivery arrangements or resolve a concern.

If a parcel appears delayed

Review the latest available tracking event and confirm the reference with the sender. If the update does not answer your question, contact JRS Express directly. Avoid relying on unofficial estimates or sharing personal shipment details in public posts.

To start checking a reference, visit the JRS Tracking tool.

Service availability, delivery schedules, restrictions and charges can change. Confirm current details with JRS Express before sending a parcel.
