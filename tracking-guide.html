Shipment basics

How to track a JRS Express shipment

A shipment reference helps a tracking service identify the parcel record. Keep the receipt until the parcel has been delivered.

1. Find the reference number

Check the receipt, waybill, or shipping document provided when the parcel was accepted. Look for the shipment or waybill reference and copy it carefully. Avoid confusing it with a payment reference or another number printed on the document.

2. Enter the number into a tracking service

Open a tracking page and enter the reference exactly as it appears. Check for accidental spaces, missing characters, or characters that look similar, such as zero and the letter O.

You can start with the JRS Tracking tool. This is an independent tracking resource, so consult JRS Express directly if you need an official confirmation.

3. Review the latest available update

Read the status and any date or location information displayed. A tracking page may not show a new event immediately after a parcel is accepted or moved. An unchanged result does not, by itself, explain why there has been no update.

4. Keep your receipt and ask for help when needed

If the reference is not recognized, recheck the characters and try again later. If the issue continues, contact the sender or JRS Express through its official channels and provide the reference from your receipt.

Privacy tip: Do not post a full waybill number, address, phone number, or recipient details publicly when asking for tracking help.
